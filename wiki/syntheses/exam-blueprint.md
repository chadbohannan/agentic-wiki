# NCP-AAI Exam Blueprint

NVIDIA's Agentic AI Professional certification (NCP-AAI) organizes the field into ten topic areas. This page maps each area to the wiki pages that cover it, which is the only place the wiki is organised by exam objective: pages are written one per concept, and one page often serves several objectives. It also records where the blueprint disagrees with itself or with the sources it cites, so the weights and references can be used with appropriate caution.

## Coverage

The study guide weights Agent Architecture and Design and Agent Development at 15% each. Architecture is covered by [Agent Architecture Patterns](../concepts/agent-architecture-patterns.md), [ReAct](../concepts/react-reasoning-action.md), [Agent Protocols](../concepts/agent-protocols-mcp-a2a.md), [Multi-Agent Orchestration](../concepts/multi-agent-orchestration.md), [Knowledge Graphs and GraphRAG](../concepts/knowledge-graphs-graphrag.md) and [Agent Interfaces and Oversight UI](../concepts/agent-interfaces-and-oversight.md) (objective 1.1). Development is covered by [Context Engineering](../concepts/context-engineering.md), which absorbs the guide's prompt-engineering objective under a name the guide does not use, [Structured Outputs](../concepts/structured-outputs.md), [Tool Calling and MCP](../concepts/tool-calling-and-mcp.md), [Multimodal Agents](../concepts/multimodal-agents.md), [Agent Error Handling](../concepts/agent-error-handling-resilience.md), [Streaming](../concepts/streaming-and-realtime-agent-ux.md), and for objective 2.6 (refining decision-making) the trajectory evaluation in [Agent Evaluation Pipelines](../concepts/agent-evaluation-pipelines.md) and the design-time fixes in [Tuning Agents for Accuracy, Latency and Cost](../concepts/agent-tuning-accuracy-latency-cost.md).

Cognition, Planning, and Memory (10%) is covered by [Agent Memory](../concepts/agent-memory.md), [Chain-of-Thought and Task Decomposition](../concepts/chain-of-thought-and-task-decomposition.md), [LLM Agent Planning](../concepts/llm-agent-planning.md), [Stateful Orchestration](../concepts/stateful-orchestration.md) and [Reflection and Feedback Adaptation](../concepts/reflection-and-feedback-adaptation.md). The guide's NVIDIA tooling for several of these areas is in [NeMo Agent Toolkit](../components/nemo-agent-toolkit.md).

Evaluation and Tuning (13%) is covered by [Agent Evaluation Pipelines](../concepts/agent-evaluation-pipelines.md), [Agent Benchmarks](../concepts/agent-benchmarks.md), [LLM-as-Judge](../concepts/llm-as-judge.md), [RAG Evaluation and Ragas](../concepts/rag-evaluation-ragas.md), [Synthetic Data for Evaluation](../concepts/synthetic-data-for-evaluation.md), [Feedback Loops and the Data Flywheel](../concepts/feedback-loops-and-data-flywheel.md), [Tuning Agents for Accuracy, Latency and Cost](../concepts/agent-tuning-accuracy-latency-cost.md), [Agent Profiling and Result Analysis](../concepts/agent-profiling-and-result-analysis.md) [NeMo Evaluator](../components/nemo-evaluator.md) and [NeMo Helix](../components/nemo-helix.md).

Deployment and Scaling is covered by [NVIDIA Dynamo](../components/nvidia-dynamo.md), [Disaggregated Serving and KV-Aware Routing](../concepts/disaggregated-serving-and-kv-routing.md), [Kubernetes GPU Inference Deployment](../concepts/kubernetes-gpu-inference-deployment.md), [NVIDIA NIM Operator](../components/nim-operator.md), [AI-Q Blueprint](../components/ai-q-blueprint.md), [LLM Inference Profiling](../concepts/llm-inference-profiling.md), [DCGM GPU Telemetry](../components/dcgm-gpu-telemetry.md), [Agent Release Management](../concepts/agent-release-management.md) and [Inference Cost and Availability](../concepts/inference-cost-and-availability.md). Objective 4.2 (MLOps and governance) has no source in the guide's list, so that page leans on practitioner posts and synthesis.

Knowledge Integration and Data Handling (10%) is covered by [RAG Retrieval Pipelines](../concepts/rag-retrieval-pipelines.md), [Vector Databases and ANN Tuning](../concepts/vector-databases-and-ann-tuning.md), [Document Ingestion and ETL](../concepts/document-ingestion-etl.md), [Structured Data Access and Text-to-SQL](../concepts/structured-data-access-text-to-sql.md) and [RAG vs Fine-Tuning vs Continued Pretraining](../concepts/rag-vs-fine-tuning-vs-pretraining.md). Both sources the guide lists for this section were unreadable, so the section rests on primary documents found by the ingest.

NVIDIA Platform Implementation (7%) is covered by [NeMo Guardrails](../components/nemo-guardrails.md), [NVIDIA NIM](../components/nvidia-nim.md), [TensorRT-LLM](../components/tensorrt-llm.md), [Triton Inference Server](../components/triton-inference-server.md), [LLM Inference Optimization](../concepts/llm-inference-optimization.md) and the toolkit page above. Objective 7.5 (multimodal pipelines) is covered by [Multimodal Agents](../concepts/multimodal-agents.md), which rests on NVIDIA's docs rather than study-guide sources, since the guide lists none for it.

Run, Monitor, and Maintain is covered by [Agent Observability and Tracing](../concepts/agent-observability-tracing.md), [NeMo Relay](../components/nemo-relay.md), [LLM Reliability Metrics and SLOs](../concepts/llm-reliability-metrics-slos.md), [Agent Failure Diagnosis](../concepts/agent-failure-diagnosis.md), [Model and Data Drift Monitoring](../concepts/model-and-data-drift-monitoring.md) and [NVIDIA Data Flywheel Blueprint](../components/nvidia-data-flywheel-blueprint.md), with regression benchmarking (8.3) and versioning and rollback (8.4) in [Agent Release Management](../concepts/agent-release-management.md), which also covers objective 4.2. Objective 8.5 (uptime, transparency and trust) has no page of its own: uptime is in [LLM Reliability Metrics and SLOs](../concepts/llm-reliability-metrics-slos.md) and [Agent Error Handling and Resilience](../concepts/agent-error-handling-resilience.md), and trust in [Audit Trails and Traceability](../concepts/audit-trails-and-traceability.md) and [Agent Transparency and Explainability](../concepts/agent-transparency-and-explainability.md). Several of this section's listed links point at LangChain and TensorRT-LLM pages that are only loosely related to monitoring, so the wiki leans on OpenTelemetry and NVIDIA documentation, and the SLO, canary and transparency material is largely inferred.

Safety, Ethics, and Compliance (5%) is covered by [Agent Security Threats and Controls](../concepts/agent-security-threats-and-controls.md), [Audit Trails and Traceability](../concepts/audit-trails-and-traceability.md), [Privacy and PII Protection](../concepts/privacy-and-pii-protection.md), [Bias and Toxicity Mitigation](../concepts/bias-and-toxicity-mitigation.md), [Layered Safety and Escalation](../concepts/layered-safety-and-escalation.md), [NVIDIA Safety Models and garak](../components/nvidia-safety-models-and-garak.md), [EU AI Act](../concepts/eu-ai-act.md), [AI Risk Frameworks and Standards](../concepts/ai-risk-frameworks-and-standards.md), [FDA AI-Enabled Medical Devices](../concepts/fda-ai-enabled-medical-devices.md) and [Model and Dataset Licensing](../concepts/model-and-dataset-licensing.md). The guide's regulatory links were stale: the EU link is the 2021 proposal rather than the adopted Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, and the FDA link is dead, so those pages were rebuilt from official current pages.

Human-AI Interaction and Oversight (5%) is covered by [Human-in-the-Loop Patterns](../concepts/human-in-the-loop-patterns.md), [Agent Interfaces and Oversight UI](../concepts/agent-interfaces-and-oversight.md) (10.1), [Feedback Loops and the Data Flywheel](../concepts/feedback-loops-and-data-flywheel.md) (10.2), [Agent Transparency and Explainability](../concepts/agent-transparency-and-explainability.md) (10.3) and [Human Oversight and Accountability](../concepts/human-oversight-and-accountability.md).

## Where the blueprint disagrees with itself

The study guide gives Deployment and Scaling 5% and Run, Monitor, and Maintain 7%, while the certification web page and the user's `notes.md` give 13% and 5%. The guide's weights sum to 87%, so at least one version is stale or wrong; neither has been confirmed with NVIDIA. The guide also links its courses with `C-FX-*` identifiers where the web page uses `S-FX-*`.

## Where the references are wrong or stale

Three of the guide's arXiv links resolve to different papers than their titles: 2502.05439 is a financial-services model-risk paper, 2310.10501 is the NeMo Guardrails paper rather than an in-context-learning paper, and 2504.15965v1 is a memory survey rather than a planning survey (the planning survey is 2402.02716). One link (BytePlus agent memory) now redirects to an unrelated page, three Medium posts returned HTTP 403, and the course pages contain only a stale 2023 workshop listing. The guide's "NVIDIA NeMo: A Scalable Generative AI Framework" repository now redirects to NeMo Speech, which focuses on audio, speech and multimodal LLMs after a repository split **[sourced]**. The per-source detail is in [sources.md](../../sources.md).

The guide's central NVIDIA tool has also moved. NVIDIA's migration guide states that the NeMo Agent Toolkit was deprecated in October 2026, with end of life by the end of 2026, and points to NeMo Relay, NeMo Helix and NeMo Fabric; see [NeMo Agent Toolkit](../components/nemo-agent-toolkit.md). The exam text was written before this, so concepts from the toolkit are likely still tested. The toolkit is not the only product to have moved since September 2025, and [NVIDIA Agentic Stack](nvidia-agentic-stack.md) maps every product the guide names to its status as of October 2026.
